MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into between the Company and the Vendor for the supply of contract labour across designated warehouse facilities, governing wage rates, service charges, compliance obligations and termination terms.

1. Scope of Services — The Vendor shall supply skilled, semi-skilled and unskilled workforce as per the approved labour structure communicated by the Company for each facility.

2. Compliance — The Vendor shall maintain valid GST registration, PF and ESIC coverage for all deployed personnel and submit renewal documentation before expiry.

3. Billing — Monthly billable schedules shall be generated by the Company's system and reconciled against Vendor-submitted invoices within the configured tolerance.
